Why Pedri Is Barcelona's Most Important Player

By George Oyefeso July 5, 2026 1 hour ago

Pedri has had an outstanding season so far, improving his numbers across the board. He has 2 goals and 9 assists in La Liga this season, and 4 goals and 11 assists across all competitions — excellent output for the position he plays.

His statistics have improved a lot from last season, and he is still performing at the top level. He is being considered one of the best midfielders in the world this season.

Now, there is a lot of argument over whether he is Barcelona's most important player. Some people say it is Yamal, because of the fluidity and chaos he adds to the team on the pitch. Others say it is Raphinha, because Barcelona's defence is heavily dependent on pressing, and that is what Raphinha offers to FC Barcelona.

Why I Think Pedri Is Barcelona's Most Important Player

There simply are a lot of things that Pedri brings to the table. Pedri is a player of great quality, and that quality has drawn him comparisons with great midfielders like Xavi and Iniesta.

Now I am going to go through the things that make Pedri arguably the most important player for FC Barcelona.

The Ability To Dictate The Tempo

In this modern generation of football, it is very vital to have someone with the ability to calm the game, because football has become more systematic.

We are in a current era of football where opposing teams put you under pressure and take the game to you, so it is key that there is someone who is press-resistant, and this is where Pedri comes into play.

Pedri gives you that calmness in midfield, the ability to dictate the tempo of the game, plus press resistance. Without these qualities in midfield, Barcelona's gameplay is noticeably less fluid if you look closely at the games.

Control In The Big Games

This is exactly where Pedri's absence is felt most: in the big games, when teams are willing to take the game to you. We have seen it several times — every time Pedri misses a big game for Barca, Barca's midfield collapses.

When Barca played against Dortmund, his absence was felt.

We have also seen other big games where Barca collapsed, or where, when Barcelona subs him off mid-match, the midfield loses the ability to retain possession of the ball. This is something you really need in big games, because opposing teams will take the game to you.

Defensive Contributions

As much as we know Pedri's ability on the ball, and how he is one of the best midfielders in that regard, we also cannot exclude his off-the-ball abilities.

Pedri is Barcelona's best midfielder when it comes to ball recovery. He averages 6.6 ball recoveries per match for Barcelona, the most in Spain. This is something the other Barcelona midfielders — including Frenkie de Jong, the club's defensive midfielder — do not do quite as well.
