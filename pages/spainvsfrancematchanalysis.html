Spain vs France Match Analysis

By George Oyefeso July 15, 2026 7 min read

It was a brilliant performance from Spain — they absolutely dominated France. Spain showed that success isn't just about having a crop of attacking talent or exciting star players; it's about being able to play as a team, and that showed throughout the match. France struggled to get near the ball, because Spain were simply too good in possession.

The domination was so complete that France had zero shots on target in the entire first half.

It all started when Spain won a penalty after Lucas Digne fouled Lamine Yamal.

Spain took the lead soon after, with Mikel Oyarzabal converting from the spot.

The second half then brought a second goal, Spain doubling their lead through a beautiful assist from Dani Olmo, finished off by Pedro Porro.

Spain went on to win the game 2-0 in sensational fashion.

Additional Facts

Spain controlled the game well and were solid defensively, and when it mattered most, they scored. They are now through to the final of the World Cup, where they will face either England or Argentina.

Special Shoutouts

Pau Cubarsí

He was spectacular today — excellent defensively, composed in possession, and he kept Mbappe silent for 90 minutes.

He finished the game with 91% passing accuracy, 75% of his passes played in the opposition half, seven defensive contributions, and won 100% of his tackles, among other standout numbers.

Overall, an incredible performance from him.

Rodri

He absolutely dominated that midfield — press resistant, composed, and defensively excellent throughout the match. Rodri has been key to Spain's defence so far in this tournament. A great performance from him.
