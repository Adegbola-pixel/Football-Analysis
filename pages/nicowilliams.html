Nico Williams Reaches Agreement On Personal Terms With Barcelona

By George Oyefeso July 5, 2026 1 hour ago

Barcelona have reportedly agreed personal terms with Nico Williams, as the club look to finally land the Athletic Club winger a year after a near-identical move collapsed at the same stage.

According to sources close to the negotiations, Williams has verbally committed to a long-term contract with Barcelona, with the framework of the deal — wages, length, and structure — already settled between the two parties.

It is a familiar position for both camps. Barcelona reached the same point with Williams last summer, only for the move to unravel before a transfer fee was ever agreed, with the winger instead signing a new ten-year deal at San Mamés.

Personal terms alone will not get this deal done. Barcelona still need to reach an agreement with Athletic Club over a fee, with the Basque side having shown no willingness to let Williams leave for anything less than his full release clause.

For now, Barcelona will view the agreement on personal terms as a meaningful first step, but one that means little until a fee with Athletic Club is actually settled.
