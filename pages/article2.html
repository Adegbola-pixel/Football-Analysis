Julian Alvarez Still Pushing For Barca Move

By George Oyefeso July 5, 2026 1 hour ago

It is no secret that Julian Alvarez wants to leave Atletico Madrid. He has publicly stated his desire to leave and pursue a move elsewhere, with his dream move said to be a switch to FC Barcelona. It has also been reported that Alvarez has turned down interest from other clubs, including PSG and Arsenal, as he focuses on doing everything possible to join the Catalan club.

The Obstacle

However, according to Fabrizio Romano, Atletico Madrid have made it clear they have no intention of selling Alvarez, repeatedly pointing to his €500 million release clause, a figure that is well beyond Barcelona's reach.

It remains to be seen how this situation will play out.

The Only Way Alvarez Could Force Through An Exit

There is, however, a legal route that could change the picture entirely. Under Article 16 of Spain's Royal Decree 1006/1985, which governs the employment relationship of professional athletes, a player is permitted to terminate his contract unilaterally, without paying his release clause or any transfer fee upfront.

In that scenario, it would fall to a Spanish labour court, rather than the release clause written into his deal, to determine what compensation Atletico Madrid are owed, weighing factors such as the sporting circumstances, the harm caused to the club, and the reasons behind the departure.

In practical terms, this means Alvarez could walk away from his contract with Atletico Madrid, which runs until 2030, and sign for Barcelona without the Catalan club having to pay the release clause or negotiate an upfront transfer fee.

Why Barcelona Are Not Considering That Route Right Now

It is easy to understand why Barcelona are not currently pursuing that option. It is a high-risk route that could unsettle the club's finances, given how uncertain the final compensation figure would be.
